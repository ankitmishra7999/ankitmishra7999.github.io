About me

I'm an M.Tech Computer Science student at IIIT Hyderabad, working at the intersection of Generative AI and systems engineering.

My focus is on building RAG pipelines that go beyond basic retrieval — thinking about retrieval quality, memory, guardrails, and real-world reliability. On the systems side, I care about fault tolerance, consistency, and performance.

I prefer implementing things from scratch over plugging in libraries I don't understand. That's shaped how I approach everything from transformers to distributed architectures.

Currently open to roles in Generative AI / RAG / Agentic AI and backend engineering.
